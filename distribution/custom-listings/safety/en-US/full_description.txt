Audio Blackbox is an audio dashcam for your pocket. Like a dashcam in a car, it keeps the last minutes of sound in your phone's memory, so if something unexpected happens in traffic or on the street, you can save the minutes leading up to it. Tap Save and those minutes become an audio file, even though you never pressed record.

Nothing is written to storage until you tap Save, and your recordings never leave your phone: the app never uploads your audio anywhere. It is not a hidden recorder: a visible notification stays up the whole time it is recording.

How it works
• Turn on continuous recording. A visible notification stays up the whole time the app is recording.
• Audio goes into a rolling buffer in RAM. When the buffer is full, the oldest audio is replaced by the newest, so it keeps running without filling your storage.
• Light on the battery: measured on a Samsung Galaxy S25 recording continuously at the Voice preset with the screen off, it used about 1.0% to 1.5% of the battery per hour. Other phones and settings will give different numbers.
• The buffer is at least 5 minutes, set in 5-minute steps. There is no fixed maximum: the app works out how much your phone can safely hold for the quality preset you picked, and shows that limit in Settings.
• Save writes the buffered audio since your last save (the whole buffer the first time) to an AAC (.m4a) file, so your files never overlap.
• Live starts a recording that keeps the buffered past and continues until you stop it. The free version saves a live recording automatically at 2 hours. The optional one-time Pro unlock removes that limit.

Quick to reach
• Home-screen widget to start or stop recording with one tap.
• Save, Live and Stop right from the notification.
• Pauses when another app takes the microphone, such as a phone call, and resumes on its own once it is free.
• Three quality presets: Voice (16 kHz mono, the longest buffer), Balanced (32 kHz mono) and High fidelity (44.1 kHz stereo). Higher quality uses more memory per minute, so the buffer gets shorter.
• Built-in player to listen, seek, share and delete your saved recordings.

Your audio stays on your phone
• Recorded audio never leaves your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics, no cloud. The only network use is Google Play's own billing library, for the optional Pro purchase.
• Audio stays in memory until you save it. Your recordings are never uploaded: saved files go to your phone's Recordings/Blackbox folder (Music/Blackbox on Android 10 and 11), where you control them.
• Open source under GPL-3.0, actively maintained, with the full source code on GitHub, so anyone can check what it does.

Record responsibly
Use Audio Blackbox for your own conversations and ideas. Recording other people may require their consent, and the rules differ between countries and states. Check your local recording laws before you record. You are responsible for how you use the app. Audio Blackbox is not an emergency service.
