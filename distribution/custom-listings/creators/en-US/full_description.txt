You only know a take was good after you played it. Audio Blackbox is a retroactive audio recorder that keeps the last minutes of sound in your phone's memory, so you can record past audio after it happened. Improvised a riff or a melody you want to keep? Said an idea out loud and lost it a second later? Tap Save and those minutes become an audio file, without having pressed record first.

Nothing is written to storage until you tap Save, and your recordings never leave your phone: the app never uploads your audio anywhere.

Made for creative work
• Musicians and songwriters: keep the riff or melody you just improvised, the jam that went somewhere, the line you sang once.
• Ideas: the thought you said out loud while walking, cooking or practicing.
• Rehearsals and sessions you are part of: what was just tried, what was just agreed.

How it works
• Turn on continuous recording. A visible notification stays up the whole time the app is recording.
• Audio goes into a rolling buffer in RAM. When the buffer is full, the oldest audio is replaced by the newest.
• The buffer is at least 5 minutes, set in 5-minute steps. There is no fixed maximum: the app works out how much your phone can safely hold for the quality preset you picked, and shows that limit in Settings.
• Save writes the buffered audio since your last save (the whole buffer the first time) to an AAC (.m4a) file, so your files never overlap.
• Live starts a recording that keeps the buffered past and continues until you stop it, so a take that started well keeps going. The free version saves a live recording automatically at 2 hours. The optional one-time Pro unlock removes that limit.

Features
• Three quality presets: Voice (16 kHz mono, the longest buffer), Balanced (32 kHz mono) and High fidelity (44.1 kHz stereo, which can use more than one microphone on phones that have them). Higher quality uses more memory per minute, so the buffer gets shorter.
• Home-screen widget to start or stop recording with one tap.
• Save, Live and Stop right from the notification.
• Pauses when another app takes the microphone, such as a phone call, and resumes on its own once it is free.
• Built-in player to listen, seek, share and delete your saved recordings.

Privacy
• Recorded audio never leaves your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics, no cloud. The only network use is Google Play's own billing library, for the optional Pro purchase.
• Audio stays in memory until you save it. Saved files go to your phone's Recordings/Blackbox folder (Music/Blackbox on Android 10 and 11), where you control them.
• Open source under GPL-3.0, actively maintained, with the full source code on GitHub.

Record responsibly
Use Audio Blackbox for your own conversations and ideas. Recording other people may require their consent, and the rules differ between countries and states. Check your local recording laws before you record. You are responsible for how you use the app.
