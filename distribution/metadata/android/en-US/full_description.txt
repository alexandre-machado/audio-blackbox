Audio Blackbox is a retroactive audio recorder: an audio dashcam that keeps the last minutes of sound in your phone's memory, so you can record past audio after it happened.

Audio is written to storage when you tap Save or start Live. The app never uploads your recordings; they stay on your phone.

Made for
• Musicians and songwriters: keep the riff or melody you just improvised, without having pressed record first.
• Journalists and interviews: a backup for the quote that came before you hit record on your main recorder.
• Students: keep the explanation you just missed, in classes where recording is allowed.
• Memory aid: replay instructions or details you were just told. Helpful for anyone who loses track of spoken information, for example people with ADHD. Not a medical device.
• Pocket audio dashcam: if something unexpected happens in traffic or on the street, save the minutes leading up to it.
• Ideas, meetings you are part of and family moments: the thought you said out loud, what was just agreed, the first word.

How it works
• Turn on continuous recording. A notification stays visible throughout recording.
• Audio goes into a rolling buffer in RAM. When the buffer is full, the oldest audio is replaced by the newest.
• The buffer starts at 5 minutes, in 5-minute steps, up to the app's safe memory limit for the selected quality. There is no product-imposed duration cap; Settings shows the current limit.
• Save writes the buffered audio since your last save (the whole buffer the first time) to a file, so your files never overlap.
• Save audio format is a free choice for Save and Live: AAC (default) or Opus (.ogg) for smaller voice files. Some players and messaging apps may not open .ogg. Without an Opus encoder, Opus is disabled: “Opus unavailable on this device.”
• Live saves the buffered past and continues in one file: with AAC, a .aac (ADTS) playable while recording; with Opus, a .ogg playable after recording ends. After an unexpected app closure, Opus audio already written may be recoverable; the ending may be lost. Live recording time is limited to 2 hours on the free plan, then the file is saved automatically. The file can exceed 2 hours because it includes the buffered past. A one-time Pro unlock removes the live time limit. Features
• Three quality presets: Voice (16 kHz mono, the longest buffer), Balanced (32 kHz mono) and High fidelity (44.1 kHz stereo, which can use more than one microphone on phones that have them). Higher quality uses more memory per minute, so the buffer gets shorter.
• Home-screen widget to start or stop recording with one tap.
• Save, Live and Stop right from the notification.
• Pauses when another app takes the microphone, such as a phone call, and resumes on its own once it is free.
• Built-in player to listen, seek, share and delete your saved recordings.

Privacy
• The app itself sends no audio or usage analytics. Google Play Billing (Play services) communicates with Google to process the optional purchase and check entitlement; its library also sends its own usage and error diagnostics. No ads, account or cloud audio storage.
• Buffered audio stays in memory until you save it or start Live. Your recordings are never uploaded: saved files go to your phone's Recordings/Blackbox folder (Music/Blackbox on Android 10 and 11), where you control them.
• Open source under GPL-3.0, actively maintained, with the full source code on GitHub.

Record responsibly
Use Audio Blackbox for your own conversations and ideas. Recording other people may require their consent, and the rules differ between countries and states. Check your local recording laws before you record. You are responsible for how you use the app.
