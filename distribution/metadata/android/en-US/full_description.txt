Audio Blackbox acts as a true audio black box for your day-to-day life.

It records continuously in a rolling circular buffer stored 100% in your device's RAM (5, 15, or 30 minutes). When something important happens, you can retroactively save the audio you just heard or start a forward recording without losing context.

KEY FEATURES:
• RAM-Only Circular Buffer: Audio is written to disk storage only when you explicitly tap to save.
• Save the Past: Retroactively export the last 5, 15, or 30 minutes of captured audio with a single tap.
• Continuous Forward Recording: Keep recording seamlessly while preserving the historical moments leading up to it.
• Smart Interruption Handling: Automatically pauses and resumes during phone calls while preserving timeline alignment.
• Integrated Offline Gallery: Listen, seek, share, and manage your saved recordings with an embedded native player.
• 100% Private & Secure: Zero cloud uploads, zero remote transcription, zero ads, and fully auditable open source architecture.
